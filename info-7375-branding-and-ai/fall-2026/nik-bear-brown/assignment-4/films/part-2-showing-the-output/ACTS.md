# ACTS.md — Muse shows the output

Film 2 of 4. Assignment 4, Part 2: complete the loop and show human-usable
outputs — the gallery, the end-to-end flow, the quality checks, the honest gap.

**Exact title:** Muse shows the output

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer understands that an "output" is not raw
data but a file a human can open, scan, and act on — and has seen the actual
files this project produced: one digest, fifteen role briefs, one run report,
plus the HTML version a non-technical person can open.

**Structure:** Four acts (the second act is the longest — it's the gallery).

- Act I — What counts (2 beats): a test for "output"; the end-to-end flow.
- Act II — The gallery (5 beats): digest; three brief cards; run report.
- Act III — Quality check (2 beats): the non-technical test; the honest gap.
- Act IV — The loop closed (1 beat): from 3,446 postings to files a person opens.

**Tone:** confident but honest; the film claims what the files prove and shows
the gap it hasn't closed. No bragging about "AI magic" — the outputs are files,
and files can be judged.

**What this film is not:** not a rebuild of Part 1's scoring logic; not a scale
story (that's film 3); not a sales pitch (that's film 4).
