# ACTS.md — Muse packages it

Film 4 of 4. Assignment 4, Part 4: the professional package — executive
summary, technical architecture, failure paths, integration points, demo
walkthrough plan — plus the series evaluation of Muse.

**Exact title:** Muse packages it

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer has seen a package that could be
shown to a client: a one-page executive summary, an architecture diagram
with failure paths, and a demo plan — and has heard the honest evaluation of
what Muse was actually useful for across this whole assignment.

**Structure:** Four acts.

- Act I — The package (2 beats): the executive summary; the architecture.
- Act II — The story it tells (3 beats): failure paths; integration points;
  the demo walkthrough.
- Act III — The pitch (2 beats): production verdict; what remains.
- Act IV — Muse evaluates Muse (1 beat): scripting, agents, analysis,
  repo-based work, film production — graded honestly.

**Tone:** professional, confident, self-aware. This film sells the work, not
the tool — and the final act turns the camera on the process itself.

**What this film is not:** not a rebuild of earlier films; not a feature
list. It's the package and the judgment.
