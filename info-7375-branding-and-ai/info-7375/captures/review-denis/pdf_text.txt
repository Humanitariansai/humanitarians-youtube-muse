Madison — User Guide & Feature Overview
DC-metro job market intelligence, powered by adversarial multi-agent AI
Madison puts a resume through a simulated hiring debate before the candidate ever applies. Two AI agents argue the candidate's
fit — a harsh hiring manager stress-testing every claim, and a candidate advocate surfacing transferable strengths — grounded
in live labor-market data. A third agent, the synthesis judge, reconciles both arguments into a fit score, a confidence rating, and a
concrete 3-step action plan.
