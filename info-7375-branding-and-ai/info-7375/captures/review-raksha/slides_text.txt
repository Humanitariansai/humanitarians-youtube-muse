=== Slide 4 ===
BRAND STRATEGY
Why threadline is different
A
vs. adaptive fashion product companies
Tommy Hilfiger Adaptive   •   Silverts   •   Reboundwear
They sell finished garments, built on internal assumptions, for one end consumer. Threadline sells the intelligence that tells any brand, including them, what to build next, grounded in verified community signals.
B
vs. other AI market intelligence tools
Brandwatch (sentiment monitoring)   •   Quantilope (automated surveys)
Monitoring tools track what people are saying. Survey tools automate a study once a team already knows what to ask. Neither tells a brand what to build. Threadline scans condition-specific signal directly and outputs a ranked, evidence-backed product opportunity, already scored.
