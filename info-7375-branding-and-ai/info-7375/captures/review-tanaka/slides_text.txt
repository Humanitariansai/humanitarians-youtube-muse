THE BRAND STORY
Why I built this.
THE PROBLEM
Every AI tool I worked with had the same failure mode. It was not wrong -- it was hollow. The output came back and something felt off. The AI did not know who it was talking to.
THE AGITATION
I started recognizing the pattern everywhere: the prompts were afterthoughts. The fallbacks did not exist. Nobody had designed how the AI should reason -- they just bolted it on and hoped.
THE SOLUTION
MentionMap was built on a different principle. Before the AI touches anything, it already knows your brand -- your history, your voice, your audience. The result is not just an answer. It is your brand's answer.
