=== Slide 12 ===
12
ORIGIN STORY
The story behind Olembic.
FRAMEWORK: OVERCOMING THE MONSTER
1
THE OVERWHELM
Random Word hit 3.9 million downloads. A weekend project I built to learn Python packaging. Suddenly there were 300 mentions a week and one person triaging. Me. On weekends.
2
THE PATTERN
I kept a private log. Three kinds of signals mattered, almost always. Reproducible bugs from real users. Adjacent tools citing me. Repeated docs-gap questions. Everything else was noise.
3
THE CATEGORY ERROR
Once I saw the pattern, I couldn’t unsee it. Every mention-tracking tool optimizes for coverage. Maintainers need triage. It’s not a feature gap. It’s a category error.
4
THE BUILD
I started Olembic that week. Bandit-weighted source scoring. RAG over public developer chatter. Five signals out of three hundred. Prototype, not shipped. Working on my machine.
Story  →  Values.  Measured  ·  Shipped  ·  Depth  ·  Open  ·  Honest
