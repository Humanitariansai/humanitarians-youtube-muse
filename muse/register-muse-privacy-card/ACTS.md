# ACTS.md — Register for Muse with a privacy.com card

Film 1 of the humanitarians channel Muse series. A practical how-to: register
for Muse (Meta's personal AI agent) without putting your real card on the
signup form.

**Exact title:** Register for Muse with a privacy.com card

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer knows exactly when Muse signup asks
for a card, how the one-dollar authorization hold works, and how to clear it
with a privacy.com virtual card capped at ten dollars — then finish setup
(name, agent name, connected apps) and start using Muse.

**Structure:** Three acts.

- Act 1 — The fork (3 beats): what Muse is and where to get it; the Meta
  account path (probably no card asked); the no-account path (card asked,
  one-dollar hold, released in about a week).
- Act 2 — The privacy.com card (4 beats): what privacy.com is; creating the
  account and the virtual card; setting the ten-dollar spend limit; using the
  card at signup and pausing it after.
- Act 3 — Finish setup (3 beats): name yourself, name your agent; connect
  Gmail and Calendar (optional); what free access gives you.

**Tone:** practical and plain-spoken; privacy-respecting without preaching.
Teardown register throughout — short sentences, no jargon left unexplained.
The film teaches one workaround for one signup step, not a philosophy.

**What this film is not:** not a review of Muse; not a pricing comparison (no
plan prices are quoted); not financial advice; not a privacy.com
advertisement. It does not claim the workaround is the only way — the easy
path is signing in with an existing Meta account.

**Source facts:**
- Muse: Meta's personal AI agent; launched 2026-09-08; US and Canada; web at
  muse.ai; iOS and Android apps; Mac app at ai.meta.com/muse/download/;
  free access with a usage limit; optional paid monthly plans. (docs/muse.md)
- Signup fork: with an existing Instagram / Facebook / Meta account, signup
  probably does not ask for a credit card. Without one, signup asks for a
  card and places a $1 authorization hold, released in about a week.
  (Bear's own signup, 2026-10-03) [record]
- privacy.com: virtual debit cards with per-card spend limits (per
  transaction, day, month, year, or total); over-limit transactions declined
  automatically; cards lock to the first merchant; cards can be paused or
  closed; free Personal tier; US-only; signup links a bank account or debit
  card and requires KYC identity verification. (privacy.com support docs)
- Muse onboarding: asks the user's name, then the agent's name, then offers
  Gmail and Google Calendar connections (optional). (witnessed with Bear,
  2026-10-03) [record]
