# FACTCHECK.md — Register for Muse with a privacy.com card

Every claim in the script was checked before writing. Unverifiable claims
were cut; hedges ("probably", "about") are kept where the source is one
person's experience.

## Verified (record)

1. Muse is Meta's personal AI agent; launched 2026-09-08; available in the
   US and Canada. → docs/muse.md [record]
2. Web app at muse.ai; iOS app on the App Store; Android app on Google Play;
   Mac app download at ai.meta.com/muse/download/. → docs/muse.md [record]
3. Free access with a usage limit; optional paid monthly subscriptions.
   → docs/muse.md [record]
4. With an existing Instagram / Facebook / Meta account, Muse signup
   probably does not ask for a credit card. Without one, signup asks for a
   card and places a $1 authorization hold, released in about a week.
   → Bear's own signup experience, 2026-10-03 [record]
5. privacy.com virtual cards carry per-card spend limits settable per
   transaction, day, month, year, or total; transactions over the limit are
   declined automatically. → support.privacy.com spend-limit docs [record]
6. Only the amount of each completed transaction is debited from the funding
   source; the limit is a cap, not a preload.
   → support.privacy.com [record]
7. privacy.com cards lock to the first merchant where they are used; cards
   can be paused or closed at any time. → privacy.com docs/press [record]
8. privacy.com has a free Personal tier; service is US-only (18+, US
   resident/citizen or authorized US business).
   → privacy.com pricing coverage; privacy.com/terms via press [record]
9. privacy.com signup: link a bank account or debit card as funding source;
   verify identity (KYC). → privacy.com official blog [record]
10. Muse onboarding asks the user's name, then the agent's name, then offers
    Gmail and Google Calendar connections (optional).
    → witnessed directly with Bear during his setup, 2026-10-03 [record]
11. $1 / $0 card authorization holds are standard card-verification practice;
    holds are reversed, typically within days to weeks depending on the bank.
    → payments industry practice (Stripe docs pattern) [record]

## Judgments (judgment)

  Bear's two-path account of signup. [judgment]
- "$10 limit" is Bear's chosen cap, presented as his recommendation, not a
  privacy.com requirement. [judgment]
- Calling the $1 hold "standard practice" generalizes from industry-wide
  card-verification norms to Meta's signup. [judgment]

  path only; it does not advise against using a real card. [judgment]
