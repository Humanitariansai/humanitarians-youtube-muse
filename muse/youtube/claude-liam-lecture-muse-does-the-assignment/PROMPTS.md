# PROMPTS.md — Muse Does the Assignment

No generation prompts. No paid generation was used in this film: all visuals
are Manim scenes written for the build, and all bookends are fixed Remotion
patterns rendered as labeled slates in the review cut (Bear's Mac renders the
real ones).

The only "prompt" in the film is the BHTF Your-Turn prompt, which is the
film's own content (a 4×4 grid exercise for the viewer), written in
`make_sheet.py` and read in full in the narration — not a generation prompt.
