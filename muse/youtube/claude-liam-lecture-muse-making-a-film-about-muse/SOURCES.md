The film's subject is Muse itself. The source is the product documentation
shipped with Muse, read in full before planning. Every claim in the film
traces to one of these; see FACTCHECK.md for the claim-by-claim verdicts.

## Primary

- `~/docs/muse.md` — what Muse is (personal AI agent, own dedicated computer,
  strictly personal), the model (Muse Spark, Muse model family), launch
  (September 8, 2026; US and Canada), surfaces (web, iOS, Android, Mac app,
  WhatsApp), tabs (Chat, Feed, Ideas, Goals, Library), artifacts, files,
  and the business model (free with usage limit; paid monthly subscription).

## Supporting

  files, connected accounts, paired devices); privacy boundaries.
