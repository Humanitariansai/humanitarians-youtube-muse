# ACTS.md — Agents: AI that does things.

An ai-explainer concept film for the humanitarians AI YouTube channel: what
"agentic" AI actually means for a normal person — where it helps, where it
goes wrong, and how to supervise it. Concept-first: every technical term
(agent, tool use, autonomy, the loop) is shown, not just named.

**Exact title:** Agents: AI that does things.

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer knows what an agent is (a brain,
tools, and a loop), the three rules for keeping one on a leash (delegate
only what you can check; approve anything irreversible; start on low
stakes), and the three ways it goes wrong (wrong instructions, compounding
mistakes, the tireless loop). No products named, no prices quoted, no
version-specific features asserted — the film teaches a supervision
framework, not a tool review.

**Structure:** Three acts.

- Act 1 — What "agentic" means (4 beats): cold open ask; the BLUF — not a
  smarter chatbot, a chatbot with hands; the three ingredients (model,
  tools, loop); the loop itself, see-think-act-check.
- Act 2 — The framework and the proof (3 beats): the three rules for the
  leash; the worked example — planning a Saturday evening with an approval
  gate before the booking.
- Act 3 — Where it goes wrong (5 beats): hidden instructions in content it
  reads; mistakes compounding across steps; the tireless loop and the
  time budget; the verdict recap; the your-turn prompt; the outro.
