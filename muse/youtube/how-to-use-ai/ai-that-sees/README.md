# AI that sees

An extra film beyond the 24-film How-to-AI series — a show-tell pre-render
package for the Humanitarians AI YouTube channel (`claude-liam`; persona
Liam, "in for Bear"). Companion to film 18 ("Just talk to it"): that film
covered when *talking* beats typing; this one covers when *showing* beats
typing.

**Pitch:** Screenshots, photos, forms — the multimodal use cases that beat
typing: receipts, error messages, plants, forms.

You can attach a photo to your message the way you'd send a picture to a
friend, and a photo often says in a second what five minutes of typing
cannot. This film shows the move (attach → the AI scans → it answers in
words), six use cases (error messages, receipts, sick plants, forms,
which-one comparisons, handwriting), three photo rules (take it like
evidence, point at what matters, always add a few words), and one privacy
rule: the AI reads everything you show it — photograph the receipt, not the
bank statement.

narration). See `ACTS.md` (argument), `SHOTLIST.md` (shots), `FACTCHECK.md`
(verified claims, 2026-10-04), `make_sheet.py` → `beat_sheet.json`,
`scenes.py`, `SOURCES.md`, `PROMPTS.md`, `BUILD-LOG.md`, `CHECKS-REPORT.md`,
and `CLAUDE-CODE-RENDER.md` (render steps for Bear's Mac).
