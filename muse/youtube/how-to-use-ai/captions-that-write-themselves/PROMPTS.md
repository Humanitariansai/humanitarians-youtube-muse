# PROMPTS.md — Captions That Write Themselves

No image, video, or audio generation prompts were used in building this
film. All visuals are original Manim drawings (isometric, Claude
palette); the bookends are the house Remotion patterns
(`BrutalistHesitantWriter`, `ClaudeDefinitions`, `ClaudeComposerAsk`,
`ClaudeTitleOutro`). Nothing here is a prompt for a generative model.

## The viewer's prompt (read in full in BHTF, shown in the composer)

This is the copy-paste prompt the film hands to the viewer. It is a
deliverable of the film, not a generation prompt.

> Find any line where the words do not make sense in context, and
> suggest the fix. List the five lines you are least sure about.

The narration frames it: "This week, caption one video. Generate the
draft, then paste it into the AI and ask:" — then reads the prompt in
full — "Then check two things yourself:"

1. Are the names and unusual words spelled right?
2. Does it read cleanly when you watch with the sound off?

The viewer runs the prompt on their own generated caption file and
judges the result themselves.
