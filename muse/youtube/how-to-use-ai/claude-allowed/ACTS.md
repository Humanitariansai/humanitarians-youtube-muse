## Reframing vs the source

- Source was K-12 ("your teacher", "grade points", "this semester's homework").
  Kept the school policy as the *concrete running example* (it is where the language is
  most standardized), but the narration names employers, and the Your-Turn beat ends
  "At work, swap in your manager." The point — scope, exception trap, disclosure — is
  identical for any AI-use policy.
- No cold-open, no verdict card (show-tell bookend exemptions). The body is six drawn
  beats; zero body cards (none passed the card test).
- No quantitative claims anywhere. The source's PEDAGOGY reserved the Bastani numbers
  for H2; this film keeps that discipline.
- House red line preserved: the film teaches read-the-policy, ask-in-writing, disclose,
  build-fluency. It never teaches evasion, detection-dodging, or ban-circumvention.
