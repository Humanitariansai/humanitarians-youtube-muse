# PROMPTS.md — Claude, Allowed.

**No generation prompts.** This film uses zero generated imagery: every visual is an
isometric Manim drawing (from the show-tell kit pasted into `scenes.py`) or one of the
fixed house Remotion bookends (`BrutalistHesitantWriter`, `ClaudeDefinitions`,
`ClaudeComposerAsk`, `ClaudeTitleOutro`).

The only prompt text in the package is the viewer's Your-Turn handoff, read aloud in
full and stored verbatim in `beat_sheet.json` (BHTF `command`):

> Draft me a short, honest email to my teacher asking whether I can use Claude for
> [describe your assignment or subject]. I want to start the right conversation —
> keep it to one paragraph.

No image model, no stock, no pantry assets were used or needed.
