# ACTS.md — "Claude, Not Your Answer." (general-audience redo)

Redo of `claude-for-artificial-intelligence/hai-not-your-answer/`
("Stop using your own Claude at work.") from the mirror repo
`nikbearbrown/humanitarians-youtube-muse`, rebuilt for the humanitarians AI
YouTube channel. Same argument, new audience: smart and pragmatic, not
necessarily AI experts. Every term is explained in plain words; the privacy
mechanism is shown, not told.

Channel: claude-liam (Liam, in for Bear · Kokoro am_onyx · Teardown
register · @NikBearBrown). Skill: **show-tell** — one drawn isometric
illustration per beat, minimal labels, the voice carries the explanation.
No cards used: every beat passed the card test as a drawing (the film's own
cast — chat window, documents, toggle, seals — shows each idea more clearly
than any card could).

**Exact title:** Claude, Not Your Answer.

**Core promise:** By the end, a non-expert viewer knows that a personal AI
account can train on their chats, knows the one toggle that stops it (in
Claude and the other three big apps), knows the toggle only works going
forward, and has a clean-room rule for what never goes in.

**What changed from the original (redo notes):**
- The original's 11 beats (mixed old-format: GREETING card, VERDICT recap,
  COLD-OPEN prompt, HANDOFF) rebuilt as 13 show-tell beats: hesitant-writer
  cold open (BIDEA), terms card (BDEFS), 9 drawn body beats, composer Your
  Turn (BHTF), spoken outro (BOUT). The old VERDICT recap is dropped per the
  show-tell bookends (bookend_exempt cold-open + bvdt).
- The cold-open prompt beat (a wall of text asking Claude to walk through the
  topic) is replaced by the hesitant writer: the naive question "Can I paste
  my work into Claude?" corrected to "Can I paste anything confidential into
  Claude?" — the film's real question, stated in one correction.
- The September 2025 Anthropic consumer-terms change (training opt-out,
  5-year retention) is now the dated spine of the mechanism beat; the
  original predates it in wording but the facts check out against it.
- Toggle paths re-verified against mid-2026 guides (see FACTCHECK.md); the
  Claude toggle is voiced generically ("the training toggle") because its
  exact label has changed wording over time.
- The legal beat keeps the source's three framings but is voiced as analysis
  with an explicit "I am not a lawyer" — not advice.
- The "Why This Matters" abstraction beat is cut; the Samsung case carries it.

**Structure:** one continuous show-tell spine (no acts).

- BIDEA — the question, corrected (hesitant writer).
- BDEFS — three terms: training, retention, clean room.
- B00 — the hero scene: you, a deadline, a paste into Claude.
- B01 — the cautionary tale: Samsung, April 2023, three leaks in ~3 weeks, ban.
- B02 — the mechanism: personal plans train by default; up to 5 years.
- B03 — the fix: Claude's Settings → Privacy toggle, flipped OFF.
- B04 — the same switch in ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini.
- B05 — the caveat: going forward only; flip it now.
- B06 — the legal reality: NDA, data law, IT policy (not legal advice).
- B07 — the clean room: four never-paste categories + anonymize.
- B08 — the company-level fix: work/enterprise plans don't train.
- BHTF — your turn: an audit prompt for your own past chats + two checks.
- BOUT — spoken outro.

**Tone:** plain, direct, pragmatic. Teardown register at its plainest: name
the mechanism, show the switch, state the rule. No scaremongering — the film
says to use Claude, just not as the place for confidential data.

**What this film is not:** not legal advice (said on screen in voice); not a
claim that any specific pasted document was ever regurgitated; not a call to
avoid AI tools.
