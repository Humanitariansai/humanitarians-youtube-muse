# PROMPTS.md — "Claude, Not Your Answer."

No generation prompts. This film uses no generated images, no stock footage,
and no AI-voiced scratch audio: every visual is a drawn Manim illustration
from `scenes.py` (show-tell style), and narration is synthesized at render
time with Kokoro voice `am_onyx` per CLAUDE-CODE-RENDER.md.

The only "prompt" in the package is the viewer's Your-Turn prompt, carried
in `beat_sheet.json` (BHTF) and read aloud in full by Liam:

> Look through our past chats and list every piece of work data I pasted
> in — names, numbers, code, meetings — and flag what should never have
> gone in.
