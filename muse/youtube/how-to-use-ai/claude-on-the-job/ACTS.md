## The argument

The source film (episode H4 of the "Claude, For Students" series) argues that
workers should stop competing with AI at recall and start conducting it at
judgment. The rewrite keeps that argument intact and reframes it for a smart,
pragmatic general audience: the student-specific framing ("Am I too late?",
classroom integrity) is gone; the working-life framing (your job, your name on
the work, a daily practice) stays.

## Why this act order

The source opened with the tier diagram as given knowledge. The rewrite earns
it: the question (BIDEA) names the anxiety, the terms (BDEFS) load the four
words the film needs, the hero (B00) shows the map, B01–B04 walk the four
levels one by one, B05 states the takeaway, B06 gives the practice, B07 draws
the boundary, B08 proves the viewer can already use the map (the quiz), and
BHTF hands them the first rep. No recap beat — the map is on screen in every
beat's cast, so nothing needs restating.
