## The film

Everyone at work is asking whether AI will take their job. Wrong race. The
real question is how to conduct the machine instead of racing it. The film
walks a four-tier map of work — machine-owned recall at the bottom, human
judgment and irreducible choices at the top — and gives the viewer a daily
practice (one real task a day, read every line, name one weak spot) and a
boundary (your name on it means your judgment owns it).

Rewritten for a smart, pragmatic general audience from episode H4 of the
"Claude, For Students" series (mirror repo
`nikbearbrown/humanitarians-youtube-muse`,
`claude-for-artificial-intelligence/hai-on-the-job/`). Every technical term
(tier, conduct, hallucination, audit) is defined aloud; the four-tier
framework is the source series' own pedagogical framing, presented as the
film's map rather than a citation.
