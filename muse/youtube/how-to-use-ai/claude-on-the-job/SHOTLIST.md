# SHOTLIST.md — "Claude, On the Job."

Every body beat is a Manim isometric drawing (Claude palette: cream stage,
warm ink, terracotta accents). Labels: 1–3 words, beside objects, never inside
an outline. Bookends are Remotion (hesitant writer, definitions, composer,
title outro).

No cards were used in the body. Card test: every beat's idea is a thing, a
part, or a flow (the tier stack, the machine, the human, the page) — never an
interface, a number, or a word — so every beat is drawn.

## Cast (kept whole-film)

- **The machine**: a dark iso block with ports.
- **You**: a small ink human figure.
- **The output**: flat white pages with ghost text lines.
- **Judgment marks**: a terracotta check / dot.
- **The tiers**: a four-block stack (dark, grey, kraft, white).
