## Thesis

Knowing when NOT to use Claude is the AI skill. Use AI for leverage — never
in place of the thing you are supposed to learn. The one question that draws
the line: *would you hide this use from your boss, your client, or your
readers?*

## Source and reframe

Rebuilt from the "Claude, For Students" season finale
(`claude-for-artificial-intelligence/hai-when-not`, beat sheet H5). The source
argument — scaffold vs. crutch, the use/don't-use lists, the hide-it test —
survives intact; everything student-specific (the H1–H5 season recap, the
"teacher or boss" classroom frame, academic-integrity policy language) is
rewritten for a smart, pragmatic general audience: bosses, clients, and
readers instead of teachers; "the drafting was the thinking" instead of
"skipping the class."
