# PROMPTS.md — "Claude, When Not."

No generation prompts. This film uses no AI image, video, or text generation
in its visuals: every frame is a hand-built Manim drawing (show-tell style)
from `scenes.py`. There is nothing to prompt.

## The one prompt in the film (the viewer's, not the builder's)

The BHTF "Your turn" beat gives the viewer a prompt to paste into Claude,
read aloud in full in the narration:

> list five things I use you for each week. Run each through the hide-it
> test — would I hide this use from my boss? Mark each one scaffold or
> crutch.

The viewer's own checks: (1) does Claude's verdict match your gut?
(2) would you tape its one-sentence summary to your laptop?

## Research / rewrite prompts used

Research was done against the source beat sheet and its PEDAGOGY.md evidence
table plus targeted web verification of the hallucination grounding (Ji et al.
2023; NIST AI 600-1; GPT-4 technical report). No prompt-engineered research
