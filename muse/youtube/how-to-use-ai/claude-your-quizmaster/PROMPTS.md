No generation prompts. No AI image, video, or copy-generation prompts
were used to make this package; every drawing is hand-authored Manim in
`scenes.py` and every narration line is hand-written in `make_sheet.py`.

## The film's your-turn prompt (given to the viewer, not to a model)

This is the prompt BHTF hands the viewer to paste into Claude. It is read
aloud in full in the BHTF narration and drawn on the composer card in M08.

> Quiz me on YOUR TOPIC. Ask one question at a time and wait for my
> answer. Tell me what is right, what is incomplete, and what I missed.
> After five questions, give me a spaced schedule: when to review again —
> tomorrow, next week, next month.

The viewer replaces YOUR TOPIC with their own material and answers each
question before looking.
