## The one number (verified 2026-10-04 via web search)

- **TechCrunch, March 2025** — "A quarter of the startups in YC's current
  cohort have a codebase that is almost entirely AI-generated": Y Combinator
  managing partner **Jared Friedman** said a quarter of the Winter 2025 batch
  had codebases that were 95% AI-generated, in the YC YouTube video "Vibe
  Coding is the Future" (with CEO **Garry Tan**, managing partner Harj Taggar,
  and general partner Diana Hu). Garry Tan repeated the figure in a later
  talk ("a quarter of the companies had code bases that were ninety-five
  percent AI-generated"). Attributed aloud in the film ("Y Combinator …
  says") and captioned on screen ("per Y Combinator"), per the show-tell
  thin-numbers law.
- Caveat noted in reporting (and respected in the film): Friedman emphasized
  these founders are highly technical — the film does not claim they were
  non-programmers. The film's claim is only that the tools are good enough for
  whole products, so a beginner's one page is within reach.
- Supporting color from the same video: general partner **Diana Hu** — "to do
  good vibe coding, you need taste and knowledge to decide what's good and
  bad" — the film's mistake-three ("click every button yourself") is the
  beginner's version of the same judgment.

## Safety guidance (mistake two)

- **Anthropic's Privacy Policy / consumer terms**: advise users not to share
  sensitive personal information in prompts; submitted content may be used for
  safety purposes and (depending on settings) training. The film's "once it's
  in, you can't take it back" reflects that submitted content leaves the
  user's control. No policy text is quoted; the film states it as a rule of
  thumb.
