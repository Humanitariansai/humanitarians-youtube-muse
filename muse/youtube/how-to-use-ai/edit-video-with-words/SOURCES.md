## YouTube conversational editing

- Announcement: Made on YouTube event, 2026-09-23 — conversational editing
  tool using natural language to refine long- and short-form footage.
  https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/youtubes-conversational-video-editing-tool-lets-creators-make-edits-in-natural-language/
- Launch window: "early 2027" for YouTube Shorts and the YouTube Create app
  (TechCrunch; corroborated by chronicle.ng, marketnewsng.com,
  cryptobriefing.com, thedailytechfeed.com, absolutegeeks.com). YouTube's
  own blog posts state no date — the film attributes the window as
  "reported".
  https://chronicle.ng/youtube-ai-tool-natural-language-video-editing/
- Demo: creator Happy Kelli — dancing footage in different outfits; asked
  the AI to select the best take for each outfit, then add music and
  transitions. Quote: "A one-hour shoot can be 10 hours or more in the
  edit." / "If at any point I want even more control, I can jump back into
  the editing timeline and manually adjust things myself." / "edits with
  you, not for you" (paraphrase of "a collaborative partner that edits with
  you, not for you").
- Capabilities: add text overlays, remove pauses in speech, make editing
  suggestions based on the context of the creator's conversation with the
  AI; creators can return to the editing timeline for manual adjustments.
- Context: "hundreds of thousands of channels" used Gemini Omni in Shorts
  and Create daily in August 2026 (YouTube, via exemplary.ai — not quoted
  in the film).
  https://exemplary.ai/blog/youtube-ai-editor-shorts
- What exists today: YouTube Create (YouTube's free editing app, Android +
  iOS) — AI first draft auto-trims and arranges clips; auto captions,
  background-noise removal, royalty-free music library (YouTube, via
  exemplary.ai). Edit with AI test in Shorts camera since Sept 2025
  (English/Hindi, select markets).

## Meta Edits Assistant

- Announced 2026-09-30; rolling out to Edits app users in the US (iOS +
  Android); broader global rollout to follow.
  https://industrywired.com/technology/instagram-edits-ai-assistant-analyzes-reels-suggests-what-to-post-next-12613417
  https://realhacker.news/instagram-launches-ai-assistant-that-analyzes-your-reels-and-suggests-new-ideas/
- What it reads: follows, views, video retention, likes, shares, comments,
  Instagram trends, audience interests.
  https://www.webpronews.com/instagrams-edits-assistant-reads-your-reels-data-to-guide-what-comes-next/
- What it suggests: video concepts, hooks, captions, scripts, trending
  audio (savable from chat); trial-Reels comparisons.
- The line: "Edits assistant does the digging for you. The creative calls
  are still yours." — Brett Westervelt, who leads Edits (Meta).
- Cost: free with a daily prompt limit; Meta One subscription (announced
  2026-09-15) to unlock more usage — not priced yet. The film does NOT
  quote pricing (standing preference); the daily limit is stated as a
  usage fact.
  https://techmymoney.com/2026/10/05/instagram-edits-assistant-is-us-only-for-now-and-its-paid-upgrade-has-no-price/
