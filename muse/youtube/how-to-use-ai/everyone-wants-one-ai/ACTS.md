# ACTS.md — "ChatGPT or Claude?" (general-audience redo)

Redo of `claude-for-artificial-intelligence/everyone-wants-one-ai/`
("ChatGPT-5.5 or Claude 4.7?") for the humanitarians AI YouTube channel.
Same film, new audience: smart and pragmatic, NOT necessarily AI experts.
Every term is explained in plain words; every beat shows rather than tells.

ai-explainer skill. Channel: claude-liam (Liam, in for Bear · Kokoro
am_onyx · Teardown register · @NikBearBrown).

**Exact title:** ChatGPT or Claude?

Retitled from the working title "ChatGPT-5.5 or Claude 4.7?". Model
version numbers churn weekly (one press cycle has GPT-5.5 entering ChatGPT
in April 2026 and leaving it in October 2026; Anthropic's line moved from
Sonnet 4.5 to Opus 4.7 to Opus 4.8 in the same span). A title with point
releases would date the film before it ships. The film names vendors and
approaches, never point releases — see FACTCHECK.md.

**Core promise:** By the end, a viewer who has never compared AI tools
understands the honest way to choose: the one rule, why benchmark charts
don't decide, where each tool is genuinely stronger, and the 30-minute
test that settles it for their own work.

**Structure:** three acts.

- Act 1 — The question, reframed (4 beats: B00–B03): cold open on the
  question itself; BLUF (stop shopping for the smartest, pick the fit);
  four terms in plain English; the one rule (use what you have, or both).
- Act 2 — Why the answer looks the way it does (4 beats: B04–B07):
  benchmarks measure what labs want measured; Claude's edge is boring —
  persistent context from a folder you write once; ChatGPT's lane is
  images, spreadsheets, deep search; the two exits (writing → Claude,
  images/sheets/research → ChatGPT).
- Act 3 — The test and the verdict (4 beats: B08, BVDT, BHTF, BOUT):
  the 30-minute same-task test; the verdict (one AI is a fantasy, the
  stack is the answer); your turn (a paste-into-Claude interview prompt);
  title outro.
