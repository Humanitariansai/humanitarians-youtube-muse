# FACTCHECK.md — "Fix your photos"

Checked 2026-10-05. No invented statistics anywhere in the film; the film's
only numbers are beat counts. Every factual claim below is generic enough to
survive UI redesigns, and the narration is hedged where a single product is
named. Nothing in the film depends on version-specific UI, pricing, or model
availability. The edits are framed as "the AI tools in your photo app" with
Google Photos as the named, verified example; the film makes NO claim that
Claude itself erases or edits photos (leaked system-prompt mirrors describe
Claude as unable to edit images except by writing code — see row 10).
