# Fix your photos

Film 39 of the How-to-AI series (Wave 6, "Making things") — a show-tell
pre-render package for the Humanitarians AI YouTube channel (`claude-liam`;
persona Liam, "in for Bear"). Companion to the extra film `ai-that-sees`:
that film covered handing the AI a photo so it can *describe* it; this one
covers *changing* it.

**Pitch:** AI cleanup of your own photos: remove the photobomber, fix the
lighting, before you print.

The edits happen in the AI tools of your photo app — Google Photos' Magic
Eraser and Enhance are the named, verified examples — not in Claude. The
film teaches object removal (the AI paints in the background it expects),
hallucination (the AI inventing the fill: fine on grass and sky, risky on
faces, hands, and words on signs), the one-tap lighting fix, straightening
the horizon and tightening the frame, and three rules: edit a copy, never
the original; zoom into the spot the AI fixed and check the edges; an edited
photo is a made thing — fine for the album and the print shop, never as
evidence, never documents. Then print the one finished file.

**Package:** 14 beats, 10 Manim scenes, ~216 s estimated (Kokoro `am_onyx`
narration). See `ACTS.md` (argument), `SHOTLIST.md` (shots), `FACTCHECK.md`
(verified claims, 2026-10-05), `make_sheet.py` → `beat_sheet.json`,
`scenes.py`, `SOURCES.md`, `PROMPTS.md`, `BUILD-LOG.md`, `CHECKS-REPORT.md`,
and `CLAUDE-CODE-RENDER.md` (render steps for Bear's Mac).
