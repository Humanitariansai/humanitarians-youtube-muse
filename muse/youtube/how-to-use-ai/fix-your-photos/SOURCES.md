# SOURCES.md — "Fix your photos"

Source: NEW — built from scratch. No mirror-repo source. The edit flow
(photobomber removal → invented fill → lighting → straighten/crop → the
three rules → print last) is the film's own craft guidance for a general
audience; it is not quoted from any source.

The photo-app AI editing tools (the film's only product facts) were verified
against these public sources on 2026-10-05 (see FACTCHECK.md for the
claim-by-claim mapping):

1. Google's Photos editing page: "To remove unwanted objects, open your
   photo, tap Edit, then Tools (iOS) or Actions (Android), and select Magic
   Eraser. You can tap identified distractions or manually circle/brush over
   items to erase them." Also: one-tap suggestions ("Enhance", "Dynamic", "AI
   Enhance"); Adjust exposes Brightness, Contrast, White Balance, Saturation,
   Shadows; Crop with Rotate; non-destructive editing with Revert and
   save-as-copy.
   https://www.google.com/intl/en_us/photos/editing/
2. ScreenRant's Pixel 7 Magic Eraser guide: the phone "will attempt to figure
   out what the background would look like if the distraction wasn't there";
   photobombs called out as the named use case.
   https://screenrant.com/pixel-7-magic-eraser-remove-photobombers-how/
3. Android Police (Magic Eraser on the Pixel Tablet): "The Pixel Tablet takes
   cues from the surroundings to fill in the erased area"; complex elements
   may not come out perfectly.
   https://www.androidpolice.com/magic-eraser-pixel-tablet-tutorial/
4. MakeUseOf: "The AI will fill in the background seamlessly once erased"
   after tap or draw gestures.
   https://www.makeuseof.com/i-tried-pixels-magic-eraser-and-stopped-using-my-editing-apps-entirely/#threads
5. A Magic Eraser step-by-step guide: "In highly detailed scenes, with many
   people or complex patterns, you're more likely to notice flaws"; advises
   zooming in to define edges and retrying when artifacts appear.
   https://en.todoandroid.es/Guide-to-using-the-magic-eraser-on-your-Google-Pixel/
6. Leaked Claude Sonnet 4.5 system-prompt mirrors — the reason the film never
   claims Claude itself edits photos: "Claude does not have the ability to
   view, generate, edit, manipulate or search for images, except when the
   user has uploaded an image." (Claude describes; it does not edit.)
   https://github.com/jujumilk3/leaked-system-prompts/blob/HEAD/anthropic-claude-sonnet-4.5-full_20250929.md

No other sources. Nothing in the film depends on version-specific UI,
pricing, or model availability, so these sources establish the durable
pattern only.
