# ACTS.md — Free vs paid: when to pay.

An ai-explainer concept film for the humanitarians AI YouTube channel: what
the paid tiers of AI chatbots actually buy you, and the two-question test
that tells you whether paying is worth it. A decision framework, not a price
list — prices go stale fast; the framework doesn't.

**Exact title:** Free vs paid: when to pay.

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer knows the four things money buys
(a sharper model, higher limits, longer memory, early features), can run the
two-question test (do I hit the wall weekly? is an hour of my time worth
more than the fee?), and knows the one trap (paying for a tier you forget
to use). No prices are quoted anywhere in the film.

**Structure:** Three acts.

- Act 1 — The wall (2 beats): the free tier is the real thing with a meter;
  why the limit exists — every answer costs the company real money, so the
  limit is the price of free.
- Act 2 — What the money buys (3 beats): the four-item menu; test one — the
  sharper model only matters on hard questions; test two — the limit
  arithmetic: the free tier costs time, and the moment your time is worth
  more than the fee, paying is a refund.
- Act 3 — The decision (4 beats): when free is enough (casual questions,
  trying things out, light use); when paying pays off (daily professional
  use, long documents, coding help, you keep hitting the limit); the
  two-question flowchart; the trap — never buy the annual plan before the
  one-month experiment.

**Tone:** Teardown at its plainest — short sentences, every term defined in
the same breath it appears, no jargon left standing. Liam's voice, warm and
direct.

**What this film is not:** not a price comparison — no prices, no named
products, no "which plan" recommendation. Not a product demo — no specific AI
product is shown or named beyond the handoff's "open the AI you use most."
Not financial advice — the "is an hour of your time worth more than the fee"
test is a thinking tool the viewer fills in with their own numbers.

**Source facts:**
- Paid consumer AI tiers (ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Gemini AI Pro and
  equivalents, observed 2026-10-03) generally offer: a stronger model than
  the free tier, higher usage allowances, longer context windows, and
  earlier access to new features. Free tiers carry usage caps (message
  allowances, compute allowances, or refresh windows). Tiers and prices
  change often — the film teaches the shape, not the numbers. [record]
- No prices are stated in the film; none needed fact-checking. [record]
