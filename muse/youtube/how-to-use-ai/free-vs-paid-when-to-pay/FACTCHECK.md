# FACTCHECK.md — Free vs paid: when to pay.

Every claim in the script was checked before writing. The film quotes NO
prices and names NO products — a deliberate design decision so the film
cannot go stale. Unverifiable claims were cut; hedges ("every paid tier
I've ever seen", "say") are kept where the source is a pattern or the
viewer's own estimate.

## Verified (record)

1. Paid consumer AI tiers offer, relative to their free tiers: (a) access
   to stronger/more capable models, (b) higher usage allowances, (c) longer
   context windows, (d) earlier access to new features. → [record]
   (gizmotimes.com "ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Claude in 2026", comparison
   table: paid tiers add premium reasoning, expanded research/uploads,
   larger context; bravenewcoin.com 2026 AI model guide pricing tables;
   both crawled within days of 2026-10-03)
2. Free tiers carry usage caps of various shapes: ChatGPT free = unlimited
   everyday text chats with heavier tools separately limited; Claude free =
   session-based allowance resetting every ~5 hours; Gemini free =
   compute-based allowance refreshing ~5 hourly with a weekly limit.
   → [record] (scriptbyai.com "AI Chatbot Free Plan Limits", updated
   2026-09-22; tech-insider.org free-tier spec table, crawled 2026-10-03)
3. Every model response costs the provider real compute (electricity, chips)
   — the economic reason free tiers are capped. → [record] (general,
   uncontested; scriptbyai.com notes limits exist because advanced features
   "cost meaningful compute")
4. Pricing tiers shift frequently: "Prices as of July 2026 … Verify before
   buying — several of these changed within the last six weeks"
   (bravenewcoin.com); derecktafuma.co.uk (2026-09-21): "Pricing on all
   three shifts more often than the models themselves do." This is the
   evidence for the film's no-prices design decision. → [record]

## Corrected / hedged (judgment)

5. B02 says "in every paid tier I've ever seen" — a soft first-person
   attribution, not a universal claim; the paid landscape includes
   edge cases (team/API plans) the film does not cover. [judgment]
6. B04's "an hour of your week … four hours a month" uses a 4-week month
   as a round example, flagged in-narration with "say". [judgment]
7. B03's "the flagship pulls ahead … clearer, fewer mistakes" is a
   generalization about stronger vs weaker models on hard tasks; the film
   frames it as a test the viewer can run, not a benchmark result. [judgment]

## Exempt (no external claim)

- The decision framework itself (four-item menu, two-question test,
  one-month-before-annual rule) is the film's own teaching device, not a
  factual claim. [record]
- "The free tier is a sample tray at a bakery" is a metaphor, labeled as
  one in the narration ("is"). [record]
