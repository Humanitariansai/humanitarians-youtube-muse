# ACTS.md — "Give it a voice"

## The film in one breath

Your slides are done, and they are silent. Instead of recording the
voiceover yourself — twenty takes into a microphone — let AI do the
talking. Suno's new Speech beta reads your script and mixes original
background music under it in one track; Google Vids already has AI
voiceovers built into the video editor. The voice reads exactly what
you wrote, typos included, so the one real skill is writing a script
worth reading aloud.

## Tone

Teardown at its plainest: direct sentences, no setup-and-payoff, no
jokes. Every term is defined in the same breath it's first used
("text-to-speech" lands in BDEFS and is shown in B01, not just named).
The demo does the persuading; the narration points at it. The limit
beat (B04) is the film's honesty — "beta means rough, and Suno's own
team says so" — and no statistic is invented anywhere. Companion to
`pictures-from-words` (the Suno music film's sibling): that one made
songs, this one makes speech.
