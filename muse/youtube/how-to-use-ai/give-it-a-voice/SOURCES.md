# SOURCES.md — "Give it a voice"

Research re-verified at build time, 2026-10-05. All Suno Speech
sources date from the launch (Oct 1–2, 2026); all Google Vids sources
from Google's own Workspace Updates blog unless noted.

## Suno Speech beta (launched 2026-10-01)

- https://aidailypost.com/news/suno-ai-music-generator-adds-integrated
  (Oct 2, 2026): public beta across web and mobile; feed in a script
  or a description, get a synthetic voiceover with the option to layer
  AI-generated music underneath automatically; music toggle on/off.
- https://www.okaynews.com/suno-speech-beta-ai-narration-background-music/
  (Oct 2, 2026): announced Oct 1, 2026 by Suno CPO Jack Brody after a
  month of testing with a small group; voice and musical style
  described by the user; narration and music placed in the same track;
  company acknowledges accent and timing flaws; no separate price or
  usage allowance announced.
- https://tpsreport.news/news/suno-speech-public-beta-ai-voice-music
  (Oct 2, 2026): two modes — Simple (describe the output in a prompt
  box, e.g. "a pirate captain rallying his crew") and Advanced
  (custom script; controls for voice gender, speech style, vocal
  variety); background music optional via toggle; max output ~8 min;
  Brody: "Beta really does mean beta. Occasionally, British accents
  can wander off to Australia and back. Dramatic pauses may be very
  dramatic."
- https://www.unite.ai/suno-launches-speech-beta-pairing-voice-and-music-in-one-model/
  (Oct 2, 2026): Suno's claim of "the first audio model that generates
  voice and music together as one cohesive track" (company claim,
  no benchmarks published); release note dated Oct 1, 2026 tags
  Android, iOS, web and Create.
- https://www.aimusicpreneur.com/ai-tools-news/suno-speech-beta-spoken-word-music-one-take-launch/
  (Oct 2, 2026): launch graphic examples "Energetic cheerleader" and
  "Victorian English with a harpsichord"; Suno's pitched uses —
  bedtime stories over soft piano, hype speeches over stadium drums,
  meditations, pep talks.
- https://the-decoder.com/ai-music-generator-suno-can-now-create-spoken-audio-with-matching-background-music/
  (Oct 2, 2026): British accent can sometimes sound Australian (beta bug).

## Google Vids AI voiceovers (already shipped — corrects the pitch)

- http://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/04/new-more-expressive-ai-voiceovers-in-Google-Vids-and-16-additional-languages-powered-by-Gemini-3.1-Flash-TTS.html
  (Apr 15, 2026): 30 new conversational voice options in Vids,
  supported in 24 languages, powered by Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS;
  emotional instructions and bracket pacing tags
  (e.g. "This [pause] is amazing!").
- http://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/06/easily-steer-ai-voiceover-and-avatar-speaking-with-emotions-pacing-and-sound-effects.html
  (Jul 16, 2026): steering tags typed in brackets like "[excitedly]";
  "Apply audio tags" auto-populates tags from script content.
- https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/google-makes-ai-video-creation-free-with-gemini-omni-in-google-vids-111282.htm
  (Sep 24, 2026): Google "plans to add Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite
  text-to-speech to Vids" — this is the upgrade that is "coming";
  the base AI-voiceover feature already exists.
