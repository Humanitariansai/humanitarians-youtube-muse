## The argument (one paragraph)

Most people type to the AI because they never noticed they can talk to it.
Voice mode starts with one tap on a microphone or waveform icon; you speak in
full sentences and the AI answers out loud, back and forth like a phone call.
Talking wins when you're thinking out loud (brainstorming), when your hands
are busy (cooking), when you need to rehearse (a hard conversation), and for
language practice. Typing wins when you need to keep something, when the
instructions must be exact, and in public. Three tips: speak in full thoughts,
interrupt freely, and ask for a summary in text when you're done.
