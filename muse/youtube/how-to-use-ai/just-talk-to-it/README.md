# Just talk to it

Film 18 of 24 in the How-to-AI series — a show-tell pre-render package for the
Humanitarians AI YouTube channel (`claude-liam`; persona Liam, "in for Bear").

**Pitch:** Voice mode basics: when talking beats typing.

Many people don't know they can just talk to the AI. This film shows how to
start a voice conversation (one tap on the mic/waveform icon — described
generically so it never goes stale), the four times voice wins (brainstorming
aloud, hands busy in the kitchen, rehearsing a hard conversation, language
practice), the three times typing wins (keep it, exactness, public), and three
tips (full thoughts, interrupt freely, ask for a text summary at the end).

**Package:** 16 beats, 12 Manim scenes, ~205 s estimated (Kokoro `am_onyx`
narration). See `ACTS.md` (argument), `SHOTLIST.md` (shots), `FACTCHECK.md`
(verified claims, 2026-10-03), `make_sheet.py` → `beat_sheet.json`,
`scenes.py`, `SOURCES.md`, `PROMPTS.md`, `BUILD-LOG.md`, `CHECKS-REPORT.md`,
and `CLAUDE-CODE-RENDER.md` (render steps for Bear's Mac).

**Status:** pre-render package only. Narration and 4K render happen on Bear's
Mac per `CLAUDE-CODE-RENDER.md`. Never publish without Bear's explicit word.
