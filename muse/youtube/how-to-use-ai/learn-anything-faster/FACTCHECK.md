## Judgments (judgment)

- The mortgage is my chosen demo topic: universally recognized, widely
  signed without being understood — the ideal "smart but new" subject.
  [judgment]
- The three-move stack (explain-it-simple / one-at-a-time quiz /
  Socratic mode) is my structure for the brief's pitch ("explanations,
  quizzes, Socratic mode"). [judgment]
- "The AI is the most patient tutor ever" is the film's framing line
  from the brief, not a measured claim — the film demonstrates patience
  (staying on a question) rather than asserting it. [judgment]
- No learning-science statistics are cited: the film makes no
  "X% better retention" style claim anywhere. [judgment]

## Cut or disclosed

- No mortgage advice is given: no rates, no lenders, no
  recommendations, no "you should" — the mortgage is only the demo
  topic (ACTS.md: "What this film is not").
- The extra-payment point is qualitative by design ("a little extra
  each month", "where does the money go first") — the exact savings
  depend on the loan, so the film quotes none.
- The BHTF prompt is presented as a starting point the viewer runs and
  judges themselves, not as a certified workflow.
- "A wrong answer is not punished. It is just not yet." is the film's
  plain-language description of the quiz move's rule, not a product
  claim about any AI's behavior.
