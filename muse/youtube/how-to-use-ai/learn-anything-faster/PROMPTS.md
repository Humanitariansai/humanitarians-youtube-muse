# PROMPTS.md — Learn Anything Faster

No image, video, or audio generation prompts were used in building this
film. All visuals are original Manim drawings (isometric, Claude
palette); the bookends are the house Remotion patterns
(`BrutalistHesitantWriter`, `ClaudeDefinitions`, `ClaudeComposerAsk`,
`ClaudeTitleOutro`). Nothing here is a prompt for a generative model.

## The viewer's prompt (read in full in BHTF, shown in the composer)

This is the copy-paste prompt the film hands to the viewer. It is a
deliverable of the film, not a generation prompt.

> Be my tutor on [your topic]. First, explain it like I am smart but
> new to it. Then quiz me, one question at a time, and do not move on
> until I get it right. When I ask why, do not give me the answer — ask
> me leading questions instead.

On screen, "[your topic]" appears as the "YOUR TOPIC" placeholder pill.
The narration reads the prompt in full, swapping "your topic" for the
bracketed placeholder, then gives the two self-checks:

1. Did you answer in your own words, before looking?
2. Did you let it stay on a question you got wrong, instead of rushing
   past it?
