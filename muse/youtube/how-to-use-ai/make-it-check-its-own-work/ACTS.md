# ACTS.md — "Make It Check Its Own Work."

## The film in one breath

After the AI answers, ask it to attack its own answer. One extra message —
"what's wrong with this?" — catches real flaws the first pass missed. It
reduces errors; it doesn't eliminate them.

## Shape

| Beat | Act job | One idea |
|---|---|---|
| BIDEA | the question | The first answer is a draft, not the answer. |
| BDEFS | terms | self-critique · assumption · objection |

| B08 | the habit | The reflex: answer, critique, decide. Two messages instead of one. |
| BHTF | your turn | The toughest-critic prompt, read aloud, plus one self-check. |
| BOUT | outro | Title restate. |

## Tone

Teardown at its plainest: direct sentences, no setup-and-payoff, no jokes.
Every term is defined in the same breath it's first used. The demo does the
persuading; the narration points at it. The limit beat (B06) is the film's
honesty — the claim is "fewer errors," never "no errors," and no statistic
is invented to dress it up.

## Redo notes

New film, no mirror source, no prior cut. Watch at the review cut: the B02
February glow must die before the B03 wave lights all twelve (never two
terracotta moments at once); the B06 ghost crack must stay visibly dim while
the magnifier passes over it.
