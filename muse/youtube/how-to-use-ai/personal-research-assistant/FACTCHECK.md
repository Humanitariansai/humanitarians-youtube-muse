## Cut or avoided

- Quotas and plan names for deep-research features (they churn) — the
  film says only "the big chat AIs" / "most chat tools".
- Benchmark scores and launch dates for deep-research products — the
  film says only what the mode does, which is stable.
- Any claim about which product invents fewer citations — not needed
  for the advice, and the numbers differ by study.
- No medical, legal, or financial advice; the film teaches *how to
  brief and read*, never what any answer is.
