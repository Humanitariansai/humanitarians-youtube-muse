# SHOTLIST.md — Your personal research assistant

Scene table: scene → beat → what the viewer sees → data source.
House style: show-tell — one isometric Manim drawing per beat on a
cream stage, minimal labels (1–3 words, beside objects, never inside),
Claude palette (STAGE #F2F0E9, INK #3D3929, TERRA #D97757). The voice
carries every idea. Same cast the whole film: the research desk (an
open iso box), source pages (iso pages), brief chips, the report page
(a flat page with claim lines and terracotta citation dots), a cursor,
date plates, and the "?" flag plate. Card test applied per beat
(see "why a card" column): every body beat is a thing, a part, or a
flow from the film's own cast — all drawings, zero cards.
