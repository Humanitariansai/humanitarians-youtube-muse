Every source behind the film. NEW — built from scratch, no mirror

## Facts checked for this film

1. Deep-research modes across the major chat AIs (ChatGPT Deep
   Research, Gemini Deep Research, Claude Research, Perplexity Deep
   Research): each browses the web, reads many sources, and returns a
   cited report —
   https://zephyr-hq.com/p/chatgpt-vs-gemini-vs-claude-deep-research
   ("You can get a finished, cited research report from three
   different AIs now"; ChatGPT searches web/files/apps and returns an
   editable document with citations; Gemini pulls web + Drive/Gmail
   into a report with sources; Claude Research covers web plus
   connected apps with citations).
2. Deep research reads dozens of pages and synthesizes them into a
   structured, cited report —
   http://dev.to/diflowrin/best-ai-research-tools-2026-what-works-5e8p
   (ChatGPT "reads and analyzes dozens of pages… includes citations
   and source links"; Perplexity "typically two to four minutes for a
   cited report").
3. Deep research takes minutes (5–30), not seconds —
   https://Www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/openais-new-deep-research-ai-surfs-the-web-writes-papers/486513
   ("Deep Research can get the same tasks done in five to 30
   minutes").
4. Generative search tools frequently provide incorrect or incomplete
   citations without disclaiming uncertainty — Tow Center for Digital
   Journalism study, summarized at
   https://www.airops.com/blog/ai-slop ("generative search tools
   frequently provided incorrect or incomplete citations—and did so
   without disclaiming any uncertainty").
5. AI-generated misinformation enters retrieval corpora, gets cited by
   AI systems, and generates new AI content repeating the claim — Lily
   Ray, "The AI Slop Loop" (published 2026-04-14),
   https://ppc.land/the-ai-slop-loop-how-fake-seo-advice-is-gaming-search-results/
   ("A documented feedback cycle in which AI-generated misinformation
   … enters the retrieval corpora of RAG-based AI systems … is cited
   as a source, generates new AI-produced content repeating the
   claim").
6. AI chatbots draw on a much narrower set of sources than a plain web
   search — Stanford study summarized at
   https://www.webpronews.com/stanford-study-ai-chatbots-create-information-bottleneck-by-citing-few-sources/
   ("chatbots can feel convenient and authoritative, they may actually
   limit exposure to diverse viewpoints"; models "referenced fewer
   than ten unique sources across dozens of responses" vs. hundreds
   from Google).
7. LLM hallucination / confabulation — standard documented behavior,
   carried from Film 21 ("Trust, but verify"), FACTCHECK item 2.
