# ACTS.md — Pictures from Words

Film 19 of the humanitarians-channel "How to AI" series. A practical how-to
for non-designers: image generation basics. This film teaches HOW TO USE an
image generator; the companion fellows film
(`fellows/rohan-v/2026-09-25-how-ai-image-generators-turn-noise-into-a-picture`)
explains HOW the technology works.

**Exact title:** Pictures from Words

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer knows the ladder for getting good
pictures out of an image generator — start vague and see what happens, add
subject + style + mood, then iterate like texting a friend — plus the
five-slot prompt recipe, what image generators are good for, and the honest
limits (text, hands/faces) and the duty to say when a picture is AI-made.

**Structure:** Three acts.

- Act 1 — The ladder (3 beats): rung one — a vague prompt gives a vague,
  generic picture; rung two — subject + style + mood transforms the result;
  rung three — iterate ("warmer light", "wider shot"), three or four rounds
  is normal.
- Act 2 — The recipe and the uses (3 beats): the five-slot recipe card
  (subject, setting, style, lighting, mood); the one-line mechanism (words
  steer a generator that starts from visual static and sharpens it step by
  step); practical uses — slides, invitations, mockups, visualizing ideas;
  drafts and placeholders are the sweet spot.
- Act 3 — The honest limits (3 beats): text inside pictures gets misspelled
  because the model draws letters rather than typing them; hands and faces
  go wrong most often — zoom in before sharing; say it's AI-made — most
  platforms offer an AI label and the label matters most when a picture
  could pass for a real photo.

**Tone:** practical and plain-spoken; encouraging, never hypey. Teardown
register throughout — short sentences, every term explained on first use.
The film teaches a skill (describing pictures in words), not a product.

**What this film is not:** not a review of any image generator; no
version-specific features are asserted, so the film does not date; not a
prompt-engineering deep dive; not legal advice on AI disclosure rules. It
does not show real AI-generated images — every "result" on screen is a
drawn Manim placeholder in house style, deliberately generic.

**Source facts:**
- Subject + style + setting + lighting + mood is the standard prompt
  formula across provider and community prompting guides (DALL-E prompting
  guides; community S-S-C-D-C method). [record]
- Text-to-image models draw letters as shapes rather than typing them;
  misspelled words in generated images are a widely documented failure
  mode (PetaPixel, 2024; TechCrunch, 2024, citing UCL and DAIR
  researchers). [record]
- Hands and fingers are a historically hard case for image generators;
  active research continues to improve them (arXiv 2408.15461 survey
  context). [record]
- Major platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Meta apps, X) carry AI-content labels;
  self-disclosure is the established norm; EU AI Act Article 50 requires
  visible labels on AI-generated content that could be mistaken for
  authentic. [record]
- Mechanism one-liner: diffusion models start from random noise (seeded)
  and remove noise step by step (Midjourney "Seeds" docs; Ho et al. 2020;
  verified in the companion fellows film). [record]
