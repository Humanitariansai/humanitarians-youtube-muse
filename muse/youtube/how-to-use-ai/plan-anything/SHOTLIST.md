# SHOTLIST.md — "Plan anything."

Every body beat is a Manim isometric drawing (Claude palette: cream stage,
warm ink, terracotta accents). Labels: 1–3 words, beside objects, never inside
an outline. Bookends are Remotion (hesitant writer, definitions, composer,
title outro).

No cards were used in the body. Card test: every beat's idea is a thing, a
part, or a flow (the loop blocks, the constraint box, the itinerary pages,
the revision arc, the checklist pills, the magnifier) — never an interface, a
number, or a word — so every beat is drawn.

## Cast (kept whole-film)

- **The plan**: white iso pages with ghost text lines (the itinerary).
- **Constraints**: small kraft chips dropping into an open kraft box.
- **Revision**: an ink arc sweep, a terracotta dot (marked), a terracotta check (done).
- **The checklist**: white pills with one-word captions, each getting a terracotta check.
- **You**: a small ink human figure.
