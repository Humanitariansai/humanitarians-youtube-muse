# SOURCES.md — Posters and Flyers

1. **Companion film** `pictures-from-words` (Humanitarians AI, film 19).
   Taught the image-generation ladder and the five-slot prompt recipe; this
   film's SOURCES inherit its two verified fact blocks: (a) text-to-image
   models draw letters as shapes — misspelled words are a widely documented
   failure mode (PetaPixel, 2024; TechCrunch, 2024, citing UCL and DAIR
   researchers); (b) major platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Meta apps, X) carry
   AI-content labels and self-disclosure is the established norm; EU AI Act
   Article 50 requires visible labels on AI-generated content that could be
   mistaken for authentic. See its ACTS.md "Source facts" and FACTCHECK.md.
2. **Paper sizes (general reference):** A4 = 210 x 297 mm; US Letter =
   8.5 x 11 in — both standard portrait printable shapes. The film asserts
   only "the tall shape paper is" and makes no claim about any generator's
   output resolution.
3. **Craft guidance:** the three-jobs poster frame, the split workflow (AI
   pictures / your words), and the print checklist (tall shape, dark on
   light, the three-second test) are original teaching material written for
   this film, not factual claims from a source.
