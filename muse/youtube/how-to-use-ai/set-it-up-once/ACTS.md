# ACTS.md — Set it up once

Film 23 of the "How to use AI" series. A practical how-to for a general
audience: stop re-explaining yourself to the AI every chat — write your
preferences down once, in the app's standing-instructions setting, and let
every new chat start informed.

**Exact title:** Set it up once

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer knows what custom instructions are
(saved lines the app reads before every chat), where the setting lives
(settings or profile, under words like "instructions", "personalization" or
"memory" — described generically so the film doesn't rot), what good
instructions look like (3–5 short concrete lines), what bad ones look like
(a life story; contradictory demands), and sees the payoff: the same
question answered with and without the instructions.

**Structure:** Three acts.

- Act 1 — The problem and the fix (3 beats): every chat starts as a
  stranger (the "explain p-values" annoyance); what standing instructions
  are (a note the app pins to every chat); where the setting lives
  (settings/profile, search for the word not the button).
- Act 2 — Write it well (3 beats): good instructions (four short concrete
  example lines); bad instructions (the life story, the contradiction);
  the payoff demo (same question, with vs. without).
- Act 3 — Keep it (3 beats): negative instructions (what you never want);
  keep it fresh (edit the note every few months); it follows you
  (same note on phone and computer).

**Tone:** practical and plain-spoken. Teardown register throughout — short
sentences, no jargon left unexplained. The film teaches one setting and one
habit, not a philosophy of AI.

**What this film is not:** not a review of any AI product; no product's
menu path is asserted as a timeless fact (all UI descriptions are kept
generic); no statistics are quoted; the "good instructions" examples are
illustrations, not claims about what any app recommends. It does not claim
instructions override everything the model does — the payoff demo is a
plausible illustration of the mechanism, stated as such.

**Source facts:**
- ChatGPT offers Custom Instructions (Settings → Personalization): two
  fields — what the app should know about you, how it should respond —
  applied to new chats. (OpenAI help docs, via web research 2026-10-03) [record]
- Claude offers Personal Preferences (Settings → General → Personal
  Preferences) plus per-project instructions (Projects). (web research
  2026-10-03) [record]
- Gemini offers "Instructions for Gemini" (Settings & help → Personal
  Intelligence) plus per-task Gems with custom instructions. (Google help
  docs, via web research 2026-10-03) [record]
- All three do the same thing in different words: standing preferences the
  user sets once, applied to new chats. The film keeps product details
  generic on purpose. [judgment]
