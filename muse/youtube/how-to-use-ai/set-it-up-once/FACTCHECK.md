# FACTCHECK.md — Set it up once

Every claim in the script was checked before writing (2026-10-03). No
statistics are quoted anywhere in the film, so there are no numbers to
verify. Unverifiable product details were kept generic by design.

## Verified (record)

1. ChatGPT offers "Custom Instructions" under Settings → Personalization
   (also reachable as "Customize ChatGPT"): two fields — "What would you
   like ChatGPT to know about you?" and "How would you like ChatGPT to
   respond?" — applied as context to new chats. → OpenAI help docs
   (help.openai.com/en/articles/8096356), corroborated by ai-toolbox.co,
   beebom.com, findskill.ai guides, 2026-10-03 [record]
2. Claude offers "Personal Preferences" (Settings → General → Personal
   Preferences), a single standing instruction inherited by every chat;
   plus per-workspace custom instructions via Projects. → web coverage
   2026-10-03 (incl. product comparison tables) [record]
3. Gemini offers "Instructions for Gemini" (Settings & help → Personal
   Intelligence) for global behavior, plus per-task Gems with custom
   instructions. → Google help docs (support.google.com/gemini/answer/
   16598625), corroborated 2026-10-03 [record]
4. The three products' features do the same thing in different words:
   standing preferences the user sets once, applied to new chats.
   → findskill.ai custom-instructions guide, 2026-10-03 [record]

## Judgments (judgment)

- The film never names a product's menu path on screen or in narration.
  Exact paths change (sources above already disagree on sub-menu names),
  so the film says "settings, or your profile" and "look for words like
  instructions, personalization, or memory". This is deliberate rot-proofing,
  not vagueness. [judgment]
- The "good instructions" examples ("keep answers short", "beginner at
  statistics", "plain English, no jargon", "steps I can try today") and the
  "never" lines are my illustrations of the pattern the guides recommend
  (short, concrete, standing answers). They are not quoted from any product's
  docs. [judgment]
- The B05 payoff demo (same question answered with and without the note) is
  a staged illustration of the mechanism, not a recorded experiment. The
  narration frames it as "the payoff" of the habit, not as a guaranteed
  outcome on any product. [judgment]
- "Most AI apps have a settings page" for this is my generalization from
  the three major products checked. The film does not claim universality.
  [judgment]
- "Review it every few months" is advice, not a product fact. [judgment]

## Cut or disclosed

- No per-product character limits, tier differences, or menu screenshots —
  all cut as version-rot bait.
- No claim that instructions override model behavior in every case; the
  film says the note "shapes" chats, not that it guarantees them.
- The BDEFS "memory" term (things the AI itself saves from your chats) is
  defined only to distinguish it from instructions; the film makes no claim
  about which products auto-save memory or how.
