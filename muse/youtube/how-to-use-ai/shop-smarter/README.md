# Shop smarter

Film 43 in the How-to-AI series (Wave 6 — Making things) — a show-tell
pre-render package for the Humanitarians AI YouTube channel (`claude-liam`;
persona Liam, "in for Bear").

**Pitch:** AI as buying co-pilot: compare options, summarize hundreds of
reviews, spot the trap in the fine print — without the AI choosing for you.

The film teaches the comparison *method*, not brand picks: start with your own
priorities, ask for a side-by-side table with one row per criterion, then ask
the trade-off question (what would I be giving up with each option). It shows
how to get the AI to summarize review patterns instead of trusting star
averages, and how to paste in the fine print to hunt money traps (warranty
gaps, return shipping, auto-renewals). One honest limit is named on screen:
the AI doesn't know today's price — always check the live price yourself.
No product endorsements; no pricing tiers quoted.

`scenes.py`, `SOURCES.md`, `PROMPTS.md`, `BUILD-LOG.md`, `CHECKS-REPORT.md`,
