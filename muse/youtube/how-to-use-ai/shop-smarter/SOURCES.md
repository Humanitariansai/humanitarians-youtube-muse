The single external fact — that fake/bought reviews are common enough to be
illegal — was verified against public sources on 2026-10-05 (see
FACTCHECK.md, claim 1):

1. FTC announcement of the final Trade Regulation Rule on the Use of Consumer
   Reviews and Testimonials (14 Aug 2024; effective 21 Oct 2024): bans fake
   reviews and testimonials, reviews by people with no experience of the
   product, and reviews bought or conditioned on sentiment.
   https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/blogs/2024/08/ftc-publishes-new-rule-banning-fake-reviews-and-testimonials
2. FTC Consumer Review Rule 2026 guide (reviewed Oct 2026): the rule covers
   AI-generated fake reviews, undisclosed insider reviews, and review
   suppression; violations carry civil penalties.
   https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/ftc-consumer-review-rule-2026-fake-reviews-testimonials-ai-social-proof
