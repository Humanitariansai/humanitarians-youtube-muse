# ACTS.md — "Show It an Example"

Film 3 of 24, How to AI. show-tell. 11 beats, 7 drawn body beats, ~209 s (3.5 min).

## The argument (one line)
One finished example teaches an AI more than a paragraph of instructions —
because examples carry length, tone, structure, vocabulary, and edge-case
handling all at once.

## Why this shape
The source lesson (Anthropic tutorial L07) argues: examples encode five
specifications simultaneously; quality beats count; one bad example
contaminates. The film keeps that argument intact and re-orders it as a
contrast the viewer can feel: the heavy rule stack (B01) vs. three light
examples (B02), then the mechanism (B03), the failure mode (B04), the
selection rules (B05), and the efficiency bonus (B06). No "why now" number
beat: the source offers no attributable figure, and law 9 (no filler) kills it.
