# ACTS.md — Small steps, big jobs

Film 11 of 24 in the "How to AI" series for the humanitarians AI YouTube
channel. A practical habit film: when a job is too big for one prompt,
break it into small steps and let each step build on the last.

**Exact title:** Small steps, big jobs

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer knows why one giant prompt
("plan my whole kitchen renovation") returns mush, and how to replace it
with a chain of small steps — budget, then layout, then materials, then
timeline — each checked, each feeding the next.

**Structure:** Two acts.

- Act 1 — The giant prompt fails (3 beats): the whole job as one giant
  box; the giant answer is mush; why it fails (a big job is really five
  small jobs wearing a trench coat).
- Act 2 — Steps that build (6 beats): step one, the budget; step two,
  the layout, built on the budget; step three, materials, inside the
  locked layout; step four, the timeline, fed by every checked answer
  (the handoff is the whole trick); the side-by-side comparison (one
  giant prompt vs. small steps); the habit to keep (one job per prompt).

**Tone:** plain and direct, Teardown register — short sentences, no
jargon left unexplained. The film teaches one habit, not a technique.
The voice explains; the drawings stay minimal (one cast of objects the
whole way: the giant box, step cards, pages, arrows, checks).

**What this film is not:** not a prompt-engineering lecture; no
statistics about model performance are claimed (there are none to
cite — this is a behavioral film); no named models are recommended;
no guarantee that step-by-step always wins on every task.
