# FACTCHECK.md — Small steps, big jobs

This is a behavioral habit film: it teaches a working method, not facts
about a product. No statistics, benchmarks, or study results are claimed
anywhere in the script. Every checkable claim is a plain description of
ordinary AI-chat behavior or the film's own advice.

## Verified / held (record)

1. The film claims that a single very large prompt tends to return a
   long, vague answer that is hard to check. → This is stated as ordinary
   experience ("Here's why"), not as a measured result. No number is
   attached. [record]
2. The film's advice: split a big job into small steps, check each
   answer, feed each answer into the next prompt. → Presented as the
   film's method ("the whole trick"), not as a universally proven
   optimum. [record]
3. Terms: "prompt" = what you type to the AI; "step" = one small job the
   AI does for you; "context" = what the AI carries from one step into
   the next. → Plain-language working definitions for a general audience;
   standard usage. [record]
4. The kitchen-renovation step order (budget → layout → materials →
   timeline) is the series brief's example. → Not presented as the only
   valid order; the film says to start with "the part everything else
   depends on". [record]
5. "Hallo" voices cleanly in Kokoro (show-tell skill, tested across the
   series). The narration avoids spelled-out acronyms, version numbers,
   and numbers spoken right before "Claude", per the skill's known
   misreading list. [record]

## Judgments (judgment)

- "Five small jobs wearing a trench coat" is the film's own metaphor for
  task decomposition. [judgment]
- The side-by-side (mush page vs. checked stack) is a dramatization of
  the method, not a screenshot of a real AI conversation. [judgment]
- The film does not claim step-by-step always wins on every task — only
  that a checkable step beats an uncheckable giant answer. [judgment]
- No "why now" statistic beat: the skill's spine allows it, but no honest
  number exists for this claim, so the takeaway beat (B07, the habit)
  takes its place. [judgment]

## Cut or disclosed

- No performance statistics of any kind are used — deliberately, per the
  task brief ("no invented statistics. Keep claims behavioral and
  modest").
- The film never promises the AI will be right; the whole method assumes
  the answers need checking ("each step is small enough to check in one
  glance").
