# ACTS.md — Talk to your tools

show-tell · Liam (in for Bear) · Teardown · claude-liam · am_onyx · 13 beats · ~4:03

Built from scratch 2026-10-04 (no mirror source). Companion to
`meetings-into-notes`. The thesis: **a connector is a plug between Claude
and one of your apps — grant it like a key, start read-only, and keep the
send button yours.** Three automations worth setting up (morning brief,
inbox triage, meeting prep) and three warnings (supervised sending, least
privilege, strangers' invites).

Skill: **show-tell** — switched from the assigned cc-explainer with
recorded reasoning (see BUILD-LOG.md): cc-explainer's TERMINAL-FIRST and
REAL-SESSION laws require a reconstructed Claude Code terminal session,
which this film does not have; it is a general-audience explainer, the
lane show-tell serves.

## Body (drawn, one image per beat)

The cast is one connected desk, carried straight through: the chat window,
the plug on its kraft cable, the email app card, the calendar page, and —
for the warnings — the SEND pill, the tray, and the stranger's invite.
Every beat is one motion on that cast.
