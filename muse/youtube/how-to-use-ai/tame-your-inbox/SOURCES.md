# SOURCES.md — Tame your inbox

## Source material (the film's argument)

Built from scratch 2026-10-05 — **no mirror source**. The thesis (use AI as
your email first-pass — triage, summarize, draft replies — and never let it
auto-send or auto-delete) was written for this film by the parent
orchestrator's brief and is craft advice, not sourced claims. Companion to
the existing film `talk-to-your-tools` (the B00 narration references it;
connectors are not re-taught per the brief).
