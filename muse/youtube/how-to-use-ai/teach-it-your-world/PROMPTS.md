# PROMPTS.md — "Teach it your world"

No generation prompts. This is an ai-explainer film: every body visual is a
hand-authored Manim drawing in `scenes.py` (iso kit + local helpers), and the
bookends are Remotion compositions (`ClaudeComposerAsk`,
`BrutalistHesitantWriter`, `ClaudeDefinitions`, `ClaudeVerdictArtifact`,
`ClaudeTitleOutro`) driven by the props in `beat_sheet.json`. Nothing was or
will be produced by an image or video generation model.

The only prompt-like texts in the package are prompts for the *viewer*:

**B00 (cold open, illustrative — not a real session):** "What should I pack
for my trek next month?" — a demonstration question; the shown answer is a
drawn generic list, not a recorded model output (see CHECKS-REPORT.md §5).

**BHTF (your turn, read aloud in full by the narration):**

> Here is our on-call rota for this month: [paste it]. From now on, answer my
> scheduling questions only from this rota, and quote the line you used. First
> question: who is on call this Friday?
