# Teach it your world

A How-to-AI series pre-render package for the Humanitarians AI YouTube
channel (`claude-liam`; persona Liam, "in for Bear").

**Pitch:** Upload your docs so AI answers from YOUR material — personal
knowledge bases, explained non-technically.

The AI knows the world's knowledge — it does not know your world. This film
shows the gap (ask about your trek, get everyone's trek), the fix (hand it
your documents: attachments, a project's knowledge shelf), the mechanism in
plain words (RAG: the AI searches your documents like a librarian and reads
only the matching pages; embeddings: similar meanings sit near each other),
what belongs in the box (your notes, work docs, only-you-know), the three
habits that make it stick (give it the source · pin it: "answer only from
these files" · make it quote), and the teardown (blind spots, stale in /
stale out, wrong doc = confident quote). Ends with a falsifiable verdict and
a paste-ready your-turn prompt.

**Package:** 13 beats, 7 Manim scenes, ~341 s estimated (Kokoro `am_onyx`
narration). See `ACTS.md` (argument), `SHOTLIST.md` (shots), `FACTCHECK.md`
(verified claims, 2026-10-04), `make_sheet.py` → `beat_sheet.json`,
`scenes.py`, `SOURCES.md`, `PROMPTS.md`, `BUILD-LOG.md`, `CHECKS-REPORT.md`,
and `CLAUDE-CODE-RENDER.md` (render steps for Bear's Mac).

**Skill:** ai-explainer (switched from the assigned cc-explainer — reasoning
in BUILD-LOG.md: cc-explainer's REAL-SESSION LAW needs a real `claude` CLI
session, which cannot exist in this VM).

**Status:** pre-render package only. Narration and 4K render happen on Bear's
Mac per `CLAUDE-CODE-RENDER.md`. Never publish without Bear's explicit word.
