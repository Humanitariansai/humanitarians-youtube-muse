# SHOTLIST — Tell the AI who to be

One drawing per body beat (show-tell law 1). Labels are 1–3 words, ink, beside
objects. Cast carried through the film: the prompt card (white page), the role
tag (white name-tag, ink text, terracotta seal dot), the Claude window (pale,
grey title band), the four register sliders, answer pages.

No ShowTellCard is used in this film: every body beat is a thing, a part, or a
flow (a tag landing, dials turning, pages splitting, cards sorted), so each one
fails the card test at step 1 — a drawing of the film's own cast teaches it
better. The four Remotion bookends (BIDEA/BDEFS/BHTF/BOUT) are the house
bookends, not cards.
