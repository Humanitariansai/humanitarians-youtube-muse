# The first answer is a draft

Film 8 of the "How to AI" series · pre-render package (everything except the final MP3/MP4).

**Pitch:** Iterate — how to push back, refine, and steer to a good result. Most people accept the AI's first answer; the power move is treating it as draft one.

**Skill:** show-tell (13 beats: hesitant-writer open, terms card, 9 drawn beats, Your-Turn composer, spoken outro) · ~150 s · 9 Manim scenes.

**The demo:** one draft page evolves across the film — "plan my dinners this week" comes back long and vague (draft 1), gets trimmed by "shorter", sharpened by "more concrete" (taco night, soup, stir-fry), and steered by "try again, but for a busy parent" (15-minute tags). Draft one vs draft three, side by side: the AI didn't get smarter — you steered it.
