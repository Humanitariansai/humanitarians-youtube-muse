# ACTS.md — "The Second Opinion"

## The film in one breath

For a big decision, never stop at the first answer. Get a second opinion:
ask a second AI, or make one AI argue against itself — steel-manning, the
strongest version of the other side, stated fairly. The second opinion
doesn't hand you the answer; it hands you the whole picture. You decide.

## Tone

Teardown at its plainest: direct sentences, no setup-and-payoff, no jokes.
Every term is defined in the same breath it's first used ("steel-manning"
lands in BDEFS and is shown in B03, not just named). The demo does the
persuading; the narration points at it. The limit beat (B06) is the film's
honesty — "a second opinion widens the view; it is not the truth" — and no
statistic is invented anywhere. Companion to `make-it-check-its-own-work`
(the one-AI self-critique film); this film is the two-opinion sibling.
