## Primary sources for the terms

1. Steel-manning — "a steel man is the opposite, of course, of a straw man...
   the art of addressing the best form of the other person's argument, even if
   it's not the one they presented" (Will Baude, Reason/Volokh Conspiracy,
   2021-05-12):
   https://reason.com/volokh/2021/05/12/steelmanning-and-interpretive-charity/
2. Steel-manning's origin — "initially gained traction within the 'Rationalist
   Movement,' a Bay-Area-centered community"; introduced to wider readership
   by Conor Friedersdorf in The Atlantic (2017) (Discourse Magazine):
   https://www.discoursemagazine.com/p/now-more-than-ever-we-need-steel-manning
3. The fair-statement rule — Daniel Dennett's first rule of a good debate:
   "attempt to express your target's position so clearly, vividly, and fairly
   that your target says things like, 'I wish I thought of putting it that
   way'" (Big Think, summarizing Dennett's Intuition Pumps, 2013):
   https://bigthink.com/mini-philosophy/daniel-dennetts-4-rules-for-a-good-debate/
4. Devil's advocate — the advocatus diaboli (Promoter of the Faith), a canon
   lawyer "appointed by Church authorities to argue against the canonization
   of a candidate... to look for holes in the evidence" (Wikipedia):
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devil%27s_advocate
   — office formally established under Sixtus V in 1587 (Britannica):
   https://www.britannica.com/topic/devils-advocate

## Research context (not quoted in the film)

5. Y. Du, S. Li, A. Torralba, J. B. Tenenbaum, I. Mordatch, "Improving
   Factuality and Reasoning in Language Models through Multiagent Debate"
   (arXiv:2305.14325, 2023): multiple model instances propose answers and
   debate over rounds to reach a more accurate consensus; the paper's protocol
   starts from independent first-round answers before any debate — the
   research cousin of this film's "keep the second take independent" beat.
   Its measured accuracy gains are deliberately not quoted on screen (they
   date fast; the audience needs the habit). https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325
