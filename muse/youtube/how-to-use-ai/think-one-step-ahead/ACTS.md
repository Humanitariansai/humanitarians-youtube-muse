## Thesis

Claude writes its answer one word at a time, left to right — so the first word
locks in everything after it. Ask for the answer straight away and Claude
defends its first guess instead of checking it. Ask it to think first, out
loud, on a scratchpad — and ask for what you'll need next — and you get
tomorrow's answer, today.
