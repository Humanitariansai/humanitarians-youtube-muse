# ACTS.md — "Video clips for free" (How to AI #35, Wave 6 "Making things")

Show-tell spine. One image per beat; the voice carries the argument.

**Core promise:** Google Vids now hands every personal Google account a free
monthly allowance of AI video clips — but the allowance is small and the
number churns. This film teaches the spending framework: what to spend a
clip on, what never to spend one on, and where to check the current number.
