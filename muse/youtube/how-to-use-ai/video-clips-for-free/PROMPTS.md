# PROMPTS.md — "Video clips for free"

No generation prompts were used for this film.

All nine body scenes are hand-authored Manim from the show-tell `iso_kit`
drawing kit (isometric boxes, film-strip frames, gauges), written directly
in `scenes.py`. The three bookends (`BrutalistHesitantWriter`,
`ClaudeDefinitions`, `ClaudeComposerAsk`, `ClaudeTitleOutro`) are the
standard show-tell Remotion components driven by `beat_sheet.json` props.

Research was conducted via web search (queries: "Google Vids free AI video
clips personal Google account Veo limits 2026"); findings are recorded in
`SOURCES.md` and verified in `FACTCHECK.md`.
