# Video clips for free

Pre-render package for the Humanitarians AI YouTube channel
(How to AI series, Wave 6 "Making things"). Channel `claude-liam` —
Liam, in for Bear. Kokoro `am_onyx`, Teardown register, watermark
`@NikBearBrown`.

**Pitch:** Google Vids now gives personal Google accounts a free monthly
allowance of AI video clips (Veo 3.1, 1080p, scene extension). Make an
announcement clip at vids.new. The free-tier number churns, so the film
teaches the spending framework and points viewers at vids.new for the
current number — no quota is hard-coded, no pricing tiers quoted.

**Skill:** show-tell (as assigned; no switch).

**Decision framework (the film's spine):** spend clips where AI wins ·
never spend on generic b-roll (use Vids' free stock) · script first, then
spend on hero shots only · extend, don't regenerate · skip the paid
extras (custom music, avatars) · check vids.new for the current number
before planning.

**Package:** 13 beats, ~3m10s estimated (190.0 s). 9 hand-authored Manim
scenes (B00–B08) + 4 Remotion bookend beats (hesitant-writer BIDEA,
terms card BDEFS, your-turn composer BHTF, locked title outro BOUT).
Zero ShowTellCards (card test documented in SHOTLIST.md).
