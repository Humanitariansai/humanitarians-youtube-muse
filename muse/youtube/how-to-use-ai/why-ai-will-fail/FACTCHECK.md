- The BMJ/penicillin framing is the film's sharpest interpretive claim: the journal was summarizing a study, not issuing an editorial dismissal. The script's wording ("reviewed the biggest study yet... and concluded it had no use beyond the laboratory") stays inside what the sources support.
- Metcalfe's "ate his words" happened in 1997 (Reuters 1997-04-11); the script says "on stage" without a year — safe.
