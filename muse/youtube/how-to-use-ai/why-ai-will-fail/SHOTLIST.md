## Visual grammar notes

- **Evidence on screen, judgment in voice.** Every quote the narration leans
  on appears verbatim on a card while Liam reacts to it; every number lands
  as a counter, bar, or banner that moves on the spoken figure.
- **Motion enacts the sentence.** The stamp slams on "wrong"; the curve
  bends on "trajectory"; the matrix checks land on "three of the four".
- **No consecutive wallpaper.** No two adjacent beats share a layout: card
  → timeline → grid → split → pair → page→dots → diagram → curve →
  columns → frame/strip → matrix → list → prompt → title.
- **PPT test.** No beat is a static headline-plus-paragraph; every beat has
  ≥2 shape-state changes across its plays (verified by the static QC gate).
