# PROMPTS.md — "Write with AI, Still Sound Like You"

No generation prompts. Every visual in this film is a hand-placed Manim drawing
(see `scenes.py`, built on the show-tell `iso_kit.py`); every word of narration is
written in `make_sheet.py` for Kokoro `am_onyx` (Liam). Nothing was generated by an
image, video, or audio model, and no generation prompts were used at any stage.

The only "prompt" in the package is the viewer's Your-Turn prompt (BHTF), which is
film content, not a production prompt:

> Here is a paragraph I wrote: [paste it]. Match this voice — my words, my rhythm,
> my sentence length. Never use these words: delve, leverage, game-changer, in
> today's fast-paced world. No em-dashes. Below are my rough thoughts, dictated.
> Clean them up without adding new ideas: [paste your thoughts].
