**Exact title:** You can't beat AI.

**Skill:** ai-explainer. One tight insight — "outsource the work, never
the understanding" — with the full bookend spine (cold open, hesitant-writer
overview, verdict, handoff, title outro).

**Framing (read first):** This film is an argument about method, not a news
report. Its central claim — you can't beat AI at producing work, so stop
competing and keep the thinking for yourself — is presented as Bear and
Claude's judgment, narrated by Liam in the Teardown register: mechanism
first, then the judgment on it. The five-step method (write constraints;
draft first; paste draft + constraints; ask for three versions; pick, edit,
own it) is the source's method, kept beat-for-beat. The GPS analogy is the
source's analogy; it is grounded by real navigation research (see
FACTCHECK.md) but the extension to AI is the film's argument, labeled as
such. The chief-of-staff scenario is illustrative — tagged on screen and in
voice ("An illustration") — never presented as a real person.

**Core promise:** By the end, a non-expert viewer can state the rule
("outsource the work, never the understanding"), run the five steps on
their own deliverable, and recognize the tell (no draft = outsourcing
understanding).
