## Verified (record)

1. GPS/spatial-memory literature: Dahmani & Bohbot (2020), Scientific
   Reports — greater lifetime GPS use associated with worse spatial memory
   during self-guided navigation (i.e. when navigating *without* GPS), with
   a longitudinal decline effect; the impairment transfers to navigation
   without the tool. The film's narration stays inside what this supports:
   "you stop learning the terrain… you're lost in your own city" and "The
   tool didn't fail. Your skill did." The film does NOT claim AI use causes
   hippocampal damage — the extension of the analogy to AI is the film's
   argument, presented as such. → https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-62877-0 [record]
2. The five-step method (constraints → draft first → paste draft +
   constraints → three versions → pick/edit/own) is reproduced exactly from
   the source beat sheet. [record]
3. The "ninety-day adoption roadmap / one-page pricing breakdown / exec
   email / by Friday / 240-person company / chief of staff" scenario is
   reproduced from the source as an *illustrative example* — the film never
   asserts these are real people or events. [record]
