## The handoff prompt (B10)

Typed into the Claude composer on screen, read aloud verbatim in the
narration, then discussed ("what it does, why it's worth running, what to
watch for in Claude's answer" — HANDOFF LAW):

> Here are my constraints and my rough draft. Work within my thinking —
> do not replace it. Give me three versions that each take a different
> approach, and tell me which assumption of mine each one challenges.

Discussion (from the B10 narration): run it on one real deliverable this
week; the point of the exercise is the contrast — watch how different the
result is when the thinking was yours first. The interesting part of the
answer is not the three versions but the challenged assumptions: each one
is a gap in the viewer's own thinking made visible.
