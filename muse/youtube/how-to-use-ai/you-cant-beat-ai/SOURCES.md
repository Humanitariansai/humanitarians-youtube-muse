  impacts spatial memory during self-guided navigation." *Scientific
  Reports*. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-62877-0 —
  grounds the GPS analogy (greater GPS reliance → worse spatial memory

  the analogy to AI as argument, not as a neuroscience claim.
