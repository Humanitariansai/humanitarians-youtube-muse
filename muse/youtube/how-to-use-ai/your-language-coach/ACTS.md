# ACTS.md — Your Language Coach

Film 44 of the humanitarians channel "How to AI" series (how-to-ai lane,
Wave 6 "Making things"). A practical how-to: conversation practice in any
language with infinite patience and instant corrections.

**Exact title:** Your Language Coach

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer knows four moves — (1) just
start talking, the conversation is the lesson; (2) stumble freely, the
coach never rushes you; (3) get every mistake corrected instantly, with
the rule explained in plain English; (4) roleplay real situations — and
gets a copy-paste coach prompt to run tonight in their own language.

**Structure:** Hook + show-tell body + closing.

- Hook (2 beats): BIDEA — the naive ask corrected ("How do I study
  French grammar?" → "How do I practice speaking French?"); BDEFS —
  three plain terms (practice / correction / roleplay).
- Body (7 beats): B00 the hero — your bubble and the coach's bubble face
  off, the conversation is the lesson; B01 patience — three stumbles, two
  gentle squiggles, one check, the coach just waits ("no clock"); B02
  instant corrections — the wrong verb is struck the moment it appears
  and the right words arrive; B03 the rule in plain English — a fix
  teaches the sentence, the rule teaches every sentence after it; B04
  roleplay — the café awning drops, you order coffee with someone
  playing along; B05 your level — "slower" shrinks the long reply into a
  short one, you turn the dials; B06 any language — hola, bonjour,
  konnichiwa, salaam pop in, plus the companion reference.
- Closing (2 beats): BHTF your-turn composer with the full copy-paste
  coach prompt; BOUT spoken outro.

**Companion:** `learn-anything-faster` (the tutor film). This film
references it in B06 ("There, a tutor. Here, a coach. Same instinct,
different game.") and shows its kraft "tutor" card sliding in beside
the coach. The companion's three moves are never re-taught.

**Source argument:** NEW — built from scratch, no mirror source. The
argument is the film's own: a language is learned by speaking it, and
the AI is the first conversation partner with infinite patience — it
waits through every stumble and corrects every mistake the instant it
happens.

**What this film is not:** not a claim about any AI product's language
list (no language counts are quoted — the viewer names their language
and tries it); not a pricing film (no tiers, no plans, nothing quoted —
per the standing rule); not a linguistics lecture (the one grammar demo,
French age taking "have" not "am", is checked against French grammar
references and stated in English so the narration stays Kokoro-clean).

**Tone:** plain and direct; Teardown register throughout. The voice
explains, the drawings show. One idea per beat, drawn once. Every
technical term is defined in the same breath it is introduced —
"roleplay" is defined in BDEFS before it is ever used.

**Skill:** show-tell (one drawing per beat, minimal labels, the voice
explains; bookends: hesitant writer, key terms, Your Turn composer,
spoken @NikBearBrown outro). Zero cards: every body beat is a drawing
of the film's own cast (your bubble, the coach bubble, word pills, the
caret and check, the rule card, the café awning, hello pills).
