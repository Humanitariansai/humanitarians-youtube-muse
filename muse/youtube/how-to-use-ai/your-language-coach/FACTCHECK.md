## Judgments (judgment)

- French is the demo language: universally recognized as "the language
  people study", and its age rule is the single cleanest
  instant-correction demo (one wrong verb, one right verb, one plain
  rule). [judgment]
- The narration speaks NO French: Kokoro voices foreign words with
  English phonemes (per the skill's greeting notes), so the rule is
  stated in English ("I have twelve years") and the hello pills
  ("hola", "bonjour", "konnichiwa", "salaam") are all words the voice
  says cleanly. [judgment]
- "Infinite patience" is the pitch's phrase and the film's framing —
  demonstrated (three stumbles, the coach unmoved) rather than measured.
  No "X% faster fluency" style claim anywhere. [judgment]
- No pricing appears anywhere: no tiers, no plans, no "free vs paid" —
  per the standing rule, the film never goes near it. [judgment]

## Cut or disclosed

- No language list is quoted: "you just name the language" keeps the
  film from dating or overstating any product's language support.
- The hello pills are flavor, not a claim: four common greetings the
  narration speaks aloud; the film does not say "the AI speaks these
  four".
- The BHTF prompt is presented as a starting point the viewer runs and
  judges themselves, not as a certified workflow.
- The companion reference is a pointer ("There, a tutor. Here, a
  coach."), not a summary — the companion's moves are not re-taught.
