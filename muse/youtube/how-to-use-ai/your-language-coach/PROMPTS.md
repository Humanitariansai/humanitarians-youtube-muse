# PROMPTS.md — Your Language Coach

No image, video, or audio generation prompts were used in building this
film. All visuals are original Manim drawings (isometric, Claude
palette); the bookends are the house Remotion patterns
(`BrutalistHesitantWriter`, `ClaudeDefinitions`, `ClaudeComposerAsk`,
`ClaudeTitleOutro`). Nothing here is a prompt for a generative model.

## The viewer's prompt (read in full in BHTF, shown in the composer)

This is the copy-paste prompt the film hands to the viewer. It is a
deliverable of the film, not a generation prompt.

> Be my French conversation coach. Speak to me mostly in French. When I
> make a mistake, correct it right away, and explain the rule in plain
> English. Start by asking me to introduce myself. If I say slower, slow
> down. If I say roleplay, become a waiter in a cafe in Paris.

Note: the prompt says "cafe" without the accent — Kokoro reads it
cleanly, and the film's on-screen label matches ("cafe").

The narration reads the prompt in full, then gives the two self-checks:

1. Did it correct you the moment you slipped, with the rule in plain
   English?
2. Did you say something in French that you would not have dared to say
   to a real person?
