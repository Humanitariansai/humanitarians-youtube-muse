# ACTS — Your song in a minute

How-to-AI film (#37) · show-tell · 12 beats · ~254 s estimated.

## Bookends (house show-tell)

- **BIDEA — The question.** Hesitant writer. Liam greets ("Hallo. This is Liam,
  in for Bear."), types the naive request ("Make me a song") and corrects it
  into the film's thesis ("Make me a happy birthday song about dinosaurs").
  The correction IS the three-ingredient ask.
- **BDEFS — Terms in this film.** AI music tool / prompt / synthetic voice,
  one line each.

## Body

- **Act 1 — The trick (B00).** The hero object: a finished birthday song
  about dinosaurs, ready in about a minute — words, music, singing, all made
  from a description.
- **Act 2 — How it works (B01).** The pipeline: your description goes into the
  music tool, it writes the music and the words, and sings them with a
  synthetic voice. Words in, song out.
- **Act 3 — What to ask for (B02).** The three ingredients: occasion
  (a birthday), subject (dinosaurs, and the kid's name), style (cheerful,
  upbeat, easy to sing along to). All three or it comes out generic.
- **Act 4 — The loop (B03–B04).** The first song is a draft: always listen to
  it all the way through — you're the only quality check the song gets. Then
  ask for a fix ("sing her name slower"), get another version. Describe,
  listen, fix; two or three rounds.
- **Act 5 — The sweet spot (B05).** Where it shines: short, personal things —
  a birthday song with the kid's name in the chorus, a jingle for your club's
  video, a lullaby from a bedtime story. One idea, two minutes, for one
  person.
- **Act 6 — The limits (B06).** Where it doesn't shine: long songs lose the
  plot halfway through; the synthetic voice can mangle a name or rhyme
  nonsense. Keep it under three minutes, and never send a song you haven't
  heard all the way through.
- **Act 7 — Why now (B07).** The number, attributed: Deezer says nearly half
  of the new music uploaded to its platform is fully AI-generated — around
  seventy-five thousand songs a day that no human wrote or played.

## Bookends (close)

- **BHTF — Your turn.** Claude.ai composer: make a cheerful birthday song for
  a seven-year-old named Mia who loves dinosaurs, under two minutes, sing her
  name clearly. Two self-checks: listen to the whole song (does the name
  come out right?); does it match all three ingredients?
- **BOUT — Spoken outro.** "Your song in a minute. At Nik Bear Brown."
