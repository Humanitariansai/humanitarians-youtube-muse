# PROMPTS — Your song in a minute

No generation prompts. This film uses no AI-generated images, video, or audio
beds: every body beat is an original isometric Manim drawing (scenes.py, house
iso kit plus this film's music helpers: note, vinyl record, song card,
music-tool window, waveform), the four bookends are Remotion patterns
(BrutalistHesitantWriter, ClaudeDefinitions, ClaudeComposerAsk,
ClaudeTitleOutro), and the narration is Kokoro `am_onyx` TTS rendered from the
beat sheet's `narration_text` on Bear's Mac. Nothing in this package requires
a text-to-image or text-to-video model.
