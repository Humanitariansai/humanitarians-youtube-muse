# SHOTLIST — Your song in a minute

One drawing per body beat (show-tell law 1). Labels are 1–3 words, ink, beside
objects. Cast carried through the film: the vinyl record (dark disc, kraft
label ring, terracotta center dot), the music note, the finished-song card
(CARD rectangle, small disc, note, ghost track lines), the music-tool window
(pale card, grey title band, note), the prompt card, name-tags, terracotta
checks, ink crosses.

No ShowTellCard is used in this film: every body beat is a thing, a part, or a
flow (a record landing, a card sliding into a window, tags dropping in, a
playhead sweeping a waveform, an arrow looping back to the tool, cards
springing up, a track wandering and fizzling, a curve climbing), so each one
fails the card test at step 1 — a drawing of the film's own cast teaches it
better. The four Remotion bookends (BIDEA/BDEFS/BHTF/BOUT) are the house
bookends, not cards.

~88%, B04's fix ~71%, B06's "under three minutes" ~68%) start their motion
