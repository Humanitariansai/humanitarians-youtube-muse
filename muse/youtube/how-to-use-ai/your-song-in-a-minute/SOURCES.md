## Product behavior (described generically, no product named in narration)

- Text-to-song AI tools (Suno, Udio and peers, 2024–2026) take a written
  prompt and return a complete song — generated music, lyrics, and vocals.
  The film describes this behavior generically ("an AI music tool") so it
  does not go stale with any one product's features or tiers.
- Known limitations reflected in B06: long-form structural coherence
  degrades, and synthetic vocals produce lyric/pronunciation artifacts —
  stated as cautions ("can mangle a name"), not benchmarks.

## Definitions (BDEFS)

- "AI music tool: an AI you describe the music you want to, and it writes the
  song" — simplified product description.
- "prompt: your written description" — standard term of art.
- "synthetic voice: a singing voice made by software, no singer required" —
  simplified accurate description.
