# ACTS.md — "Muse making a film about Muse" (general-audience redo)

Redo of `muse/youtube/claude-liam-lecture-muse-making-a-film-about-muse/`
for the humanitarians AI YouTube channel. Same film, new audience:
smart and pragmatic, not necessarily AI experts. Every term is explained
in plain words; every beat shows rather than tells.

Lecture skill. Channel: claude-liam (Liam, in for Bear · Kokoro am_onyx ·
Teardown register · @NikBearBrown).

**Exact title:** Muse making a film about Muse

The title is the premise, kept from the original: this film about Muse was
made by Muse. BIDEA nods to that once, plainly, then gets on with the
subject.

**Core promise:** By the end, a viewer who has never used an AI agent
understands what Muse is, what it does for them, where they reach it, and
how it's paid for — in plain words, with every term explained.

**What changed from the original (redo notes):**
- Same 4-act spine (what it is / what it does / where you reach it / how
  it's paid for) and same bookends.
- Terms redefined for non-experts: "agent", "memory", "skill", "artifact"
  each get a one-line plain definition in BDEFS, then are used plainly.
- "Muse Spark" is named once and immediately translated ("Meta's family
  of AI models, the engine behind the agent") — no model-family lore.
- Every beat is drawn, not carded: the 1:1 pairing, the notebook, the
  plug-ins, the document being built, the shelves, the clock, the app
  surfaces as drawn skins (SHOW-THE-THING as faithful sketches; no
  captures were taken).
- No prices, no tier names — same as the original. "Free with a limit /
  paid monthly for more" only.

**Structure:** Four acts.

- Act I — What it is (4 beats): your own agent on its own computer;
  strictly personal; Muse Spark in one line; launched Sept 8 2026, US/Canada.
- Act II — What it does (6 beats): talks; remembers; uses tools; builds
  artifacts; keeps your files; works while you're away.
- Act III — Where you reach it (4 beats): muse.ai; iPhone/Android apps;
  Mac app; WhatsApp.
- Act IV — How it's paid for (3 beats): free with a usage limit; optional
  paid monthly subscription; signup via muse.ai or app stores, monthly
  renewal, cancel anytime.

**Tone:** plain, warm, pragmatic. Teardown register. No hype ("personal
superintelligence" never appears), no jargon left standing.

**What this film is not:** not a review; not a comparison with other AIs;
not the security critique (that's the companion film); not a setup
tutorial (see "Register for Muse with a privacy.com card").
